In Saint-Martin, we vote on Saturday

As in the other French Caribbean territories, voting takes place the day before mainland France. Keep this in mind if you are setting up a proxy vote, or if you live on the Sint Maarten side while being registered on the French side.

The rules we follow

  • No paid advertising, no “boosted” posts on social media.
  • No donations or contact details collected on this site.
  • No new campaign messages from midnight on the eve of the vote.

These rules come from Nouvelle Énergie's sharing charter (in French) and the French electoral code.
