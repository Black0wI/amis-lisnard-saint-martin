About this site

Independent initiative supporting David Lisnard and Nouvelle Énergie: not run or approved by the movement. The site is published on a voluntary, non-professional basis by residents of Saint-Martin. It does not use Nouvelle Énergie's official logo or visuals.

Publisher

Jean-Baptiste Monin, Saint-Martin.

Hosting

GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States.

Personal data

This site sets no cookies, uses no audience measurement tools and contains no forms. It collects no personal data.

Three third-party services are called when you visit: Google Fonts for typefaces, Wikimedia Commons for the photos, and Nouvelle Énergie's public calendar file on the Get involved page. Like any web server, they receive the visitor's IP address. GitHub, the host, may keep technical connection logs.

Sources and rights

Quotes and measures from Nouvelle Énergie's programme each link to their original page; on the English pages they are our own translation. Figures on Saint-Martin come from IEDOM and the European Commission. The photo of David Lisnard on the home page is by Grégor Sauvagère (October 2026), published under a CC BY-SA 4.0 licence on Wikimedia Commons. The view of Marigot from Bellevue is by Thierry Pierrard, under the same licence, and the view of the marina from Fort Louis is by Richie Diesterheft (CC BY-SA 2.0); both are blurred and tinted for use as backgrounds. Texts written by our group may be freely reused with credit to the site.

Election rules

The site incurs no expenses and uses no paid advertising. No new campaign content is published from midnight on the eve of a vote.
