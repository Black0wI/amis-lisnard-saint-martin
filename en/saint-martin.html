Saint-Martin first

Eight proposals for the island

Nouvelle Énergie's national programme has no chapter on the overseas territories. Here are our group's proposals. They come from local supporters, are open for discussion, and are not commitments from the candidate.

1Local freedom

A right to adapt national rules to the island

The facts
Saint-Martin already has its own powers, such as taxation and town planning, but many national rules are still designed for mainland France, on a 53 km² island shared with Sint Maarten. EU fact sheet
What the programme says (our translation)
A “massive transfer of powers” to local governments and “autonomy of local regulatory power”, to “adapt public policies to concrete and specific local needs”. Programme, pillar 3 “Sparking a civic renewal” (in French)
Our proposal for Saint-Martin
Give the Collectivité a right to adapt national rules, at the request of the Territorial Council, with a short deadline for the State to reply.
Who decides
The State, through a revision of the organic law on the island's status.
2Water

Drinking water, a top priority

The facts
Drinking water cost €5.51/m³ in 2022, against a national average of €2.11/m³ in 2021. Both are average prices for drinking water alone: sanitation, the fixed charge and the higher band (above 30 m³ per quarter) come on top of the bill. About 30% of the water is lost through leaks in the network. EU fact sheet
What the programme says (our translation)
The national programme has no specific measure on water. This proposal builds on the decentralisation and lighter regulation it stands for. Programme, pillar 3 “Sparking a civic renewal” (in French)
Our proposal for Saint-Martin
Make the network and desalination a top priority, with a public target for cutting losses and a progress report published every year.
Who decides
The Collectivité and its water authority, with support from the State.
3Jobs

Make Saint-Martin a priority hiring zone

The facts
The 2023 census counts 27% unemployment, and the working population fell by 11.8% between 2013 and 2023. Yet salaried employment reached a record 8,119 employees at the end of 2025. IEDOM
What the programme says (our translation)
“A system of negative employer contributions will be created for hiring people who have been on the RSA for more than 2 years.” Programme, pillar 2 “Achieving a new French ambition” (in French)
Our proposal for Saint-Martin
Roll out this measure first in Saint-Martin, with training for jobs in tourism and construction.
Who decides
The State (social contributions). How it fits with the Collectivité's own taxes remains to be studied.
4Housing

Owning your own home

The facts
28.3% of households owned their home in 2020, against a national average of 63.6%, and 13.9% of homes stood empty in 2018. EU fact sheet
What the programme says (our translation)
The programme would let people “release a capitalised share” of their retirement savings “to buy their main home”, and takes housing policy beyond “social housing alone”. Programme, pillar 2 “Achieving a new French ambition” (in French)
Our proposal for Saint-Martin
Help resident families become homeowners and bring empty homes back onto the market, in line with the Local Housing Programme 2025-2030.
Who decides
The Collectivité (planning, housing) and the State (savings, grants).
5Security

Step up OFAST's work on the island

The facts
The French Senate describes an open border with Sint Maarten, with gaps in the legal framework and real security issues. Senate, 2026 OFAST, the national anti-drug office, has a unit in Saint-Martin, reinforced in 2025 with specialist officers. Outremers360, 2025
What the programme says (our translation)
Against drug trafficking: “local anti-drug units under the authority of the prefect”, “stronger international cooperation, dedicated task forces in the overseas territories”. Programme, Security page (in French)
Our proposal for Saint-Martin
Rather than adding another structure, durably reinforce the OFAST unit already on the island, make it part of the local unit the programme places under the prefect, and step up cooperation with the Sint Maarten police.
Who decides
The State, with the Netherlands for cooperation.
6Border

Firm on immigration, with discernment

The facts
Movements between the two sides of the island are not checked. Senate, 2026
What the programme says (our translation)
“Systematically expel foreign nationals convicted of serious offences” and make non-contributory benefits conditional on “at least five years” of legal residence. Programme, Immigration page (in French)
Our proposal for Saint-Martin
Focus the State's action in Saint-Martin on these two measures, on an island where many families have roots all over the Caribbean.
Who decides
The State.
7Health

A hospital run closer to home

The facts
The Louis-Constant-Fleming hospital went on strike in June 2026 and struggles to attract health workers. Senate, June 2026
What the programme says (our translation)
“Abolish the ARS, transfer responsibilities to prefects and elected officials, give mayors back the chairmanship of hospital boards”, and “double the number of medical students by 2030”. Programme, Health page (in French)
Our proposal for Saint-Martin
Saint-Martin has no mayor: the President of the Collectivité plays that role, and already chairs the hospital's supervisory board (as was the case in 2023, Soualiga Post). The measure's real benefit lies elsewhere: transferring to this board and to the prefect the decisions now taken by the ARS of Guadeloupe, and opening placements for medical interns on the island.
Who decides
The State (by law), then the Collectivité and the prefect.
8Schools

Two pilot schools for autonomy

The facts
The Collectivité is opening two secondary schools (collèges) in 2026, with 1,500 places. Collectivité, 2026 address
What the programme says (our translation)
Autonomy for head teachers to “recruit their teaching teams, apply their methods, manage their budget”, and a 20% pay rise for teachers. Programme, Education page (in French)
Our proposal for Saint-Martin
Make them pilot schools for autonomy, with a French-English bilingual project suited to the island.
Who decides
The State (national education), with the Collectivité as owner of the buildings.

Sources checked on 27 November 2026. Quotes from the programme are our own translation from French; each links to the original official page.
