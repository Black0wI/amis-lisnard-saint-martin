Saint-Martin supports David Lisnard

We are residents of the French side of the island, supporting David Lisnard and his party Nouvelle Énergie for the 2027 French presidential election.

Who we are

This is an independent supporters' initiative. It is not run, approved or funded by Nouvelle Énergie. The party's official website is unenouvelleenergie.fr.

David Lisnard is the mayor of Cannes. His programme rests on three words: liberty, order and dignity. It calls for a state focused on security, justice, education, diplomacy and health, and for a radical transfer of powers to local governments.

What we ask for Saint-Martin

  • The right for the Collectivité to adapt national rules to the island.
  • Water as a priority: it costs more than twice the national average.
  • A priority hiring zone, with 27% unemployment in 2023.
  • A permanent anti-drug task force based on the island.

These are proposals from our local group, not commitments from the candidate. Read them in French.

Make sure you are registered to vote on service-public.gouv.fr.
